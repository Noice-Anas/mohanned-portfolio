About

Hi, I'm Mohanned.

Technology has always fascinated me, not because of the software itself, but because of how it solves real business problems.

My career started in technical support and customer success, where I built a deep understanding of customer pain points and product usability. From there I moved into operations, CRM architecture, product operations, business analysis, and e-commerce, working across nearly every stage of a product's lifecycle.

Today I bridge business and technology, translating business requirements into scalable digital solutions. Whether it's building a CRM from scratch, designing dashboards, or launching an online store, I take ownership from idea to execution.
